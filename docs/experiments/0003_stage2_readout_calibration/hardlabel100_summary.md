## Label Target

Hard labels are generated by comparing each subset VGGT-native depth/pose/point-map output against the same images in the full-view VGGT cache.
The training target is the per-scene z-scored sum of `pose_rotation_mean_deg`, `pointmap_rmse_norm`, and `depth_log_rmse`.
